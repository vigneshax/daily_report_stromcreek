## Analysis: where improvement is needed

1. **Accuracy is DOWN week-over-week and the Oct 12–30 plateau (~91.7%) has not held.** W45 (11/2–11/6) is 86.5% vs ~91.7% for W42–W44. The dip is concentrated on **11/2 (81.0%) and 11/3 (81.1%)**; 11/4–11/6 recovered to 88–93%.
2. **11/2–11/3 spike looks like a new PSST-handling regression.** Of the OCR/PO flags on those two days, the top comments are "PSST was not captured" (8+4), "Incorrect order type captured (according to new PSST details)" (5), and incorrect PSST / ship date (4). Driven mostly by **Leaderpromos (17 flagged)** and **Black Rock Branding (10)**. Suggest checking what changed in PSST/order-type logic around 11/2 and adding PSST-detection cases for these two accounts.
3. **Color/Size is now the #1 theme (20.2% of flagged vs 11.8% earlier)** and Product/Part ID is steady at ~12%. Part-ID not captured / SKU not available in PPC (e.g. 1821-NY vs 1821S-NY; JT/BK vs gray) recurs for Helm, BDA Inc. Many are PO Issues (size/color not stated on PO) — consider a rule for defaulting vs. flagging missing sizes rather than silently picking one.
4. **Improvements that stuck:** Ship To/Address (24% -> 13.5%), STC (11.4% -> 3.8%), PO Number (9.4% -> 2.9%) and PO Contact Email (6.3% -> 0.6%) have all fallen sharply versus earlier flagged orders.
5. **Still-open themes:** Account Number / Shipping Acct ID (13.8%, flat) and Company Name (8.3%, nearly doubled vs earlier; e.g. 11/6 Helm "Harry Franks" captured in both ship-to and company name).
6. **Accounts to look at:** Leaderpromos (74 orders, 77.0%, down from 85.5% all-time; account number + ship method), Black Rock Branding (57 orders, 77.2%; Part ID), Dubow Textiles (70%, Company Name), BDA Inc (77.3%; Part ID not captured + order type when "Sample" is only in the email file). Helm Promotions - MI has recovered strongly (67.7% all-time -> 83.9% last 14d) but is still below average.
7. **New "OCR / Email Format" category** is trending up (0 in Sept -> 20 in W44; 27 in last 14d) — reviewers are now tagging email-body-vs-PO conflicts separately; worth a dedicated fix (read order type/notes from the email file).
8. **Associates:** accuracy is uniform (87.7–93.6%) so the errors are systemic (OCR/PO parsing), not reviewer-driven.
