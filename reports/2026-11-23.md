The contact-name error appeared 8 times this week and 0 times the week before, mostly on Helm, though not on 11/20. Brandability also dropped, from 100% to 84%.

7. **STC is rising (7 in the last 5 days, up from 3).** False-positive validation flags are still high (37, up from 35), led by Company Name (8) and PO number (8). Another client escape on 11/20 (TID 319816): the account note "Must call for CC on every order" was missed. Consider surfacing account notes during review.
