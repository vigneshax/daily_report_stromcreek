## Context: fixes already deployed (Action Tracker)

These went to prod during this window and line up with the accuracy jump after 9/4:
- Reporting Group (RG) mapping fixed to use the correct RG file instead of the customer detail list — deployed 9/3.
- STC (Shipping-To-Credit) not being captured — fixed by mapping to distributor address in customer details — deployed 9/3.
- Shipping account number casing (should be captured in caps) — deployed 9/4.
- PO Number ASB: was pulling the wrong of two PO references — deployed 9/4.
- Ship To address bleeding into the second address line — deployed 9/4.

## Where improvement is still needed

1. **Helm Promotions - MI (23.5% accuracy, 34 orders)** — the single worst account, driven by two recurring, PO-template-specific patterns rather than random noise:
   - 9/3 batch: unnecessary Reporting Group (Facilis/AIM) captured + shipping account ID not captured when the PO lists two shipper account IDs.
   - 9/9 batch: PO Contact Email not captured + wrong ship method/shipping account ID.
   This looks like the same class of fix already applied for Geiger and 4imprint in the "Distributor Wise issues" sheet — worth a distributor-specific parsing rule for Helm.
2. **Match-Up Promotions, Staples Promotional Products, The MRL Group** (65–66% accuracy) are the next tier below the ~76% overall average and don't yet have a tracked action item.
3. **Ship To/Address (21.3%) and Account Number/Shipping Acct ID (15.5%) are still the two largest error themes** even after the September fixes — the STC/RG fixes addressed part of this family but address-field and shipping-account-ID extraction remains the top OCR gap.
4. **PO Contact Email extraction** (9.6% of flagged orders, concentrated in the Helm 9/9 batch) has no corresponding entry in the Action Tracker yet — looks like a net-new gap, not a regression of something already fixed.
5. Day-to-day accuracy is volatile (62–88% swings) rather than steadily climbing — 9/23 dipped back to 71.4%, so the recent fixes have raised the average but haven't yet stabilized it.
