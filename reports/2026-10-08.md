## What changed since the last report (2026-09-25)

- Volume is up (~170/day vs ~125/day) and accuracy has **slipped from the 80–85% plateau of weeks 38–40 to 74.1% in week 41**. 10/5 was the worst day since 9/9 (69.9%, 43 OCR issues).
- Mix of errors shifted. In 10/1–10/7 (188 flagged orders), top themes are Ship To/Address (69, 37%), Product/Part ID (35, 19%), PO Number (25, 13%), Company Name (21, 11%). STC and Reporting Group are now small (9 and 4) — consistent with the 9/3 fixes holding. Product/Part ID and Company Name are **rising** (e.g. "Part id was not captured" ×11, "Incorrect Preferred Ship Date captured" ×5 are the top repeated comments this week).
- New low-accuracy accounts this week (min 8 orders): Halo Branded Solutions 57% (28), Innovative Office Solutions SD 53% (15), Brandability 50% (8), American Solutions for Business 72% (57 — high volume).
- Over the full window, worst accounts: Helm Promotions - MI 23.5% (no new orders since last report), Boundless Network 54.2%, Staples Promotional Products 65.9%.
- Associates (this week): Waman Jagdale 63%, Ashitosh Navadkar 66%, Tauqir Tamboli 69% are lowest; Sandhya Jeswani 91%. Orders are flagged by the reviewer, so differences may reflect PO mix as much as reviewer strictness — treat as a signal, not a verdict.
- Data hygiene: 70 rows have a blank "Issue related" (37 of them are "Duplicate", others "No PO"/"Rejected"); they are excluded from the flagged counts but included in the accuracy denominator. 2 rows are labelled "OCR / Email Format" and are not counted as an issue category. Consider a category for duplicates or excluding them from accuracy.

## Where improvement is needed (action list)

1. **Ship To / Address extraction** — still #1 theme (24.8% overall, 37% this week). Typical failure: a name/PO ref (e.g. "14 west PO 13735-2", a person name) captured in both ship-to and address line 2 / company name. The 9/4 fix did not fully resolve it.
2. **Part/Product ID not captured** — new top repeated comment this week; investigate PO formats from Halo, Innovative Office Solutions, and ASB.
3. **Preferred Ship Date captured incorrectly** — new recurring pattern (not in the Action Tracker).
4. **Account/Shipping account ID** — 13% overall; many "All Ok" comments cite account number missing in customer detail list (data issue, not OCR).
5. **Accounts**: add distributor-specific rules for Helm (still open), Boundless Network, Staples, Halo, Innovative Office Solutions.
6. Investigate 10/5 spike (OmniSource Marketing and Halo had 6 flagged each).
