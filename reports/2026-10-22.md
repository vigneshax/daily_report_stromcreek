## Where improvement is still needed (analyst notes, 2026-10-22)

Data note: 97 of 5,190 logged rows have a blank "Issue related" value (counted as not accurate above); 9 are tagged "OCR / Email Format". Both are excluded from the category counts in the daily table.

1. **Biggest win:** accuracy is 88–93% in the last two weeks (10/12 on), vs ~77% in the prior 5 weeks. OCR Issue count dropped from 20–43/day to 4–12/day.
2. **Error mix has shifted.** Ship To/Address was 25.4% of flagged orders before 10/12 and is now 6.0%, so the 9/4 address fixes held. In the last two weeks the top themes are Color/Size (20.7%), Account Number/Shipping Acct ID (19.8%), Product/Part ID (14.7%) and Ship Method (13.8%).
3. **PO Issues are now a larger share of misses** (43 of ~116 flagged in the last two weeks; 10 on 10/21 alone, the highest day). Many are "size/color/qty/ship method not on PO, processed with default". These are not OCR defects, but worth a rule with the client for missing size, color and ship method.
4. **Account Number / Shipping Acct ID** is still a repeat OCR gap (19.8% of recent flags), e.g. Proforma Albrecht & Co (M2410807 captured wrongly, PSST not captured), Grand Concepts, Safeguard Tri-State.
5. **Part ID / line & part quantity** misses are recurring on high-volume accounts: 4Imprint ("OCR failed to capture part id for 2XL and 3XL", part quantity not captured), Halo Branded Solutions (wrong line/part quantity, 10/21), PromoCentric (part id not captured for lines 5–10).
6. **Reporting Group over-capture (Facilis/AIM)** still appears on single orders (MSP Design Group, Corporate Recognition, Safeguard Tri-State on 10/21) despite the 9/3 RG fix.
7. **Account-level hot spots (full window):** Helm Promotions - MI at 22.9% (24 of its 35 misses came on 9/3 and 9/9; only 3 since, so likely largely resolved), Ataly Graphics 45.0% (20 orders; wrong size/part ID on multi-line POs, spread across September–October, still active), Proforma Albrecht & Co 63.2%. O C Tanner Company is 60% over the last two weeks (10 orders).
8. **Associate view:** Dhiraj Patil is at 71.7% over 566 orders, versus 79.7–87.2% for the rest. Almost all of those orders date from the earlier, lower-accuracy period (his order count barely moved since the 9/25 report), so this is not a current signal. Sandhya Jeswani (87.1%) and Ajinkya Jam (87.2%) lead.
