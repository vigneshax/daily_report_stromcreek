## Where improvement is needed (analyst notes for this run)

1. **OCR errors have gone up three weeks running, even though accuracy hasn't moved.** Weekly OCR error rate: 4.3% (w/o 10/26) → 4.5% → 5.5% → 6.4% (this week, 2 days in). Overall accuracy is still around 91% only because PO Issues dropped (69 → 47). OCR-flagged orders: 89 in the last 7 days vs 67 in the 7 before. This is the first sustained increase since the big jump around 10/12.
2. **Part ID / line not captured is the theme growing fastest (8 → 18 in 7 days).** It shows up across 4Imprint ("did not capture line for 3120, entered 11 new lines"), Halo, Staples ("Part ID not captured for line record 1-5"), Positive Promotions and Kaeser & Blair. Over all time, Geiger, ASB and 4Imprint log the most of these. It's worth checking whether a recent change hurt multi-line or table parsing.
3. **New PSST rules aren't handled in extraction.** Reviewers flagged "Incorrect order type / preferred ship date was captured (according to new PSST details)" 3 times on 11/17 (Leaderpromos, Raven Print, Black Rock Branding) and on Gott Marketing on 11/16. PSST orders are mostly Leaderpromos (102) and Black Rock Branding (53), so the order-type and ship-date logic probably needs updating for the new PSST spec.
4. **Account Number / Shipping Acct ID is still the #1 OCR theme (26 in 7 days, +5).** It's spread across many small distributors and often appears together with STC not captured (A Printers Ink, Dryvve, Go Promo, Hobert). STC went up too (3 → 8).
5. **A person's name is being captured as both Ship To and Company Name.** There were 48 mentions since 10/1, 25 of them in the last 14 days, mostly Custom Ink (12) and Helm (9). Fixing this would help Helm the most: it's the lowest-accuracy large account (72.7% all time, 82.2% this week) and has had OCR errors on 5 of the last 14 days.
6. **The Color / Size increase (+10) is mostly catalog availability, not OCR.** Most of these comments say "X is not available in PPC, processed with Y" (for example, Helm 1821 Navy goes to 1821S). Consider a substitution map such as 1821→1821S, or have reviewers tag these as PO Issue so they don't inflate the OCR error rate.
7. **4Imprint is the steadiest source of errors.** It had OCR errors on 11 of the last 14 logged days, which makes sense as the highest-volume account. The themes are mixed (account number, ship method, quantity, lines), so look at it alongside items 2 and 4 rather than giving it a separate fix.
8. **Halo ship method:** a reviewer noted "not sure about USPS due to new update, processed with UPS Ground". Halo had ship-method OCR errors on 5 of the last 14 days, so check whether a recent ship-method mapping change caused this.
