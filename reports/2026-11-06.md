## Where improvement is needed (analyst notes)

- **Regression in week 45**: accuracy fell from ~90.7% (weeks 42–44) to 83.6%. 11/2 (79.4%, 296 orders) and 11/3 (78.9%) were the worst days since early October; 11/2 had 30 OCR + 23 PO issues. Recovered to 90.5% on 11/4, 86.5% on 11/5. Check what was deployed/changed ~10/30–11/2.
- **Leaderpromos** drove most OCR/PO flags on 11/2–11/3 (17 flagged orders), followed by Black Rock Branding (10) and 4Imprint (6). 4Imprint flags are mostly ambiguous PO content (color, order type, ship method).
- **PO Issues are rising as OCR issues fall** (e.g. 10/26: 14 PO issues vs 6 OCR; 11/2: 23 PO). Dominant PO-side themes: missing ship-to address (falls back to distributor address), unavailable/unspecified color, drop-ship requests, incomplete UPS shipper numbers. These are largely not fixable by OCR; they need business rules (e.g. color-substitution table, drop-ship handling) or distributor-side follow-up.
- **Top OCR themes overall**: Ship To / Address (21.5% of flagged), Color/Size (13.9%), Account Number (13.0%), Product/Part ID (10.4%). Address and account-number extraction remain the largest fixable targets.
- **Weakest accounts**: Dubow Textiles (42%), Ataly Graphics (57%), Helm Promotions - MI (65.5% on 116 orders — highest volume among weak accounts; its flags are repeated "color not available" substitutions).
- **Associates**: Dhiraj Patil is lowest at 72.0% (576 orders); worth checking whether volume is skewed toward harder accounts or early-period work before concluding anything.
- **Data quality**: 129 rows have a blank "Issue related" and 38 are tagged "OCR / Email Format" (28 "Storm Creek Issue"); these count as not-accurate in the headline number. "Secondary Cat for OCR Issue" is almost empty (~110 of 842 OCR issues filled), so root-cause categorization relies on AX Comments keywords. Filling that column would improve this report.
