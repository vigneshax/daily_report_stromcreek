## Analyst notes (manual addendum)

- **Recent dip, not a steady improvement.** The "improved since start" headline compares against the worst early days. Accuracy peaked at 85.6% (09-30) and has since fallen: 74.0% (10-01), 77.9% (10-02), **69.9% (10-05, lowest since 09-09)**, 74.0% (10-06). 10-05 had 43 OCR issues, the highest single-day count in the window.
- **Ship To / Address regression is the main driver.** OCR-flagged Ship To/second-line errors: 56 in 667 orders since 10-01 (8.4%) vs 30 in 1,199 orders from 09-21 to 09-30 (2.5%). Other OCR themes (account number, STC, reporting group, color/size) are flat or lower in the same comparison. Suggest checking whether a recent deploy touched Ship To / second-line parsing.
- **Helm Promotions - MI: 23.5% accuracy (34 orders).** Almost all on 09-03 and 09-09. Repeated: PO Contact Email not captured, incorrect shipping account ID, wrong ship method, and an unnecessary Reporting Group (Facilis). Worth a distributor-specific fix.
- **Other low accounts:** Boundless Network 56.5%, Staples Promotional Products 65.9%.
- **Data quality:** 67 rows (16 since 10-01) have a blank "Issue related" value and 2 are tagged "OCR / Email Format". Blanks count as not accurate in the figures above, so accuracy may be slightly understated. Reviewers should fill these in.
- Associates with small volumes (Waman Jagdale, Ashitosh Navadkar, Tauqir Tamboli, under 120 orders each) show 61-65% accuracy vs 78% for the top-volume associate, though accuracy here reflects the OCR output as much as associate performance.
