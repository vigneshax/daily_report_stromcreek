## Where to act today

1. **Accuracy slipped this week.** Last 5 working days averaged 90.3% overall and 93.4% OCR accuracy, down 2.3 points on both from the prior 5 days. OCR-attributable issues doubled (84 vs 41) while volume grew only 33% (1,278 vs 960 orders). 11/13 was the worst day (25 OCR issues, 91.4% OCR accuracy). The month-level trend is still strong: Sep 83.0% → Oct 91.5% → Nov 94.7% OCR accuracy.
2. **Account number / shipper account ID is the top OCR error (23 in last 5 days, up from 13).** The Action Tracker's "Account Number Flag" (P1) is still *Not started*. It's the highest-value fix to pick up next. Examples: the system captured `8318`/`6716`/`12121` or the distributor's name in place of the PO's account number.
3. **The new PSST order-type rules aren't in the OCR yet.** 21 orders in the last 5 days have the comment "Incorrect order type / preferred ship date was captured (according to new PSST details)" but are labelled *Accurate Orders*, so they don't show up in the accuracy figure. Counting them, last-5-day OCR accuracy is about 91.7%, not 93.4%. Updating order type and ship date to the new PSST rules would clear most of this.
4. **Contact name copied into both Ship To and Company Name**: 4 of 15 OCR issues on 11/19 (Helm ×2, Brandability, Geiger). It has recurred since 10/1 (52 occurrences, about 29 of them labelled Accurate) and is now concentrated in Helm POs. A rule to keep Company Name from repeating the ship-to attention line would remove it.
5. **Helm Promotions - MI is the account to watch**: 72 orders, 13 OCR issues, accuracy down from 88% to 78%. Its errors are contact name in company name (6), Part ID/line (5) and order type (2). "Helm Shipping Method/Carrier" is still *In progress* in the tracker. Brandability also fell, from 100% to 83% (23 orders).
6. **Ship method/carrier (12, up from 6) and STC (8, up from 2) rose this week.** Halo Branded Solutions is 5 of the ship-method misses. The 11/19 client-reported escapes (TIDs 319551/319552) are **price entered as quantity** ("1 piece, 30 entered"). Check the quantity/price column mapping on those POs.
7. **False-positive flags (35 this week, up from 29)**, mostly Company Name (11), PO number (6) and Ship To (5). Tuning these validations would save reviewer time.
