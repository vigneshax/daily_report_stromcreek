## Where improvement is needed (analyst notes, 2026-11-03)

1. **Latest day (11/2, Mon) dropped to 80.7% vs 91.3% over the prior 7 days (-10.6 pts)** on the highest volume yet (290 orders). OCR issues jumped from ~3/day to 31 and PO issues hit a high of 23. The drop is spread across all 7 associates (flagged 5–13 each), so it points at a system or input change rather than one person.
2. **PSST handling is the new OCR cluster on 11/2.** Four orders had "PSST not captured" leading to wrong Order Type, Shipping Account ID and Ship Method, and two more had an incorrect order type after new PSST details. Also: ship date wrong (2), part ID not captured (2), recipient name captured into both ship-to and company name (2), RG captured incorrectly. Worth checking whether anything was deployed or whether PO formats changed over the weekend.
3. **PO Issue spike (23 on 11/2; 16 on 10/26)**: mostly PO-side data problems (ship-to missing so distributor address used, unavailable colors, wrong product IDs, 5-digit UPS shipper number). These are not OCR defects, but the volume is climbing, so ask Storm Creek about PO quality. Also confirm reviewers apply the "PO Issue" label consistently.
4. **Colour/Size and Product ID/SKU are the rising OCR themes** (share of flagged orders: Color/Size 11.5% -> 20.7%, Product ID 10.5% -> 15.2%, Company Name 4.6% -> 8.6%). Ship To/Address, STC, PO Number and PO Contact Email all fell after the September fixes. Ship To/Address is still the largest theme overall (22.3%).
5. **Accounts**: Helm Promotions - MI improved a lot (23.5% -> 80.0%) but is still 5/10 flagged on 11/2. Overall worst: Dubow Textiles (35.3%, 17 orders), Ataly Graphics (58.3%), Proforma Albrecht & Co (65.4%), Nadel (69.0%). New on 11/2: Leaderpromos 6/6 and Black Rock Branding 4/4 flagged (both were ~90% before). Small declines recently at Gator Garb, BDA Inc, Leaderpromos.
6. **Weekly trend**: W36–37 ~71%, W38–41 ~78–83%, W42–44 ~91–92%, W45 (one day) 80.7%. The gains since W42 have held for three weeks, so 11/2 should be watched on 11/3 to see whether it persists.
